
# Project Charter — E-commerce Sales Intelligence

**Project Start Date:** August 10, 2026  
**Project Status:** In Progress  
**Analyst:** Grace Tambi

## 1. Project Title

E-commerce Sales Intelligence

## 2. Background

An online retail company has experienced continuous growth in customer orders but lacks clear visibility into its sales performance. Management wants to better understand revenue trends, customer purchasing behaviour, and product performance to support data-driven business decisions.

This project uses the UCI Online Retail dataset to explore these business questions and present relevant findings through an interactive dashboard. The company represents the business scenario for this portfolio project.

## 3. Business Problem

The company collects sales data but does not have a clear understanding of which products and customers contribute most to business performance or how sales change over time. Without this information, management may struggle to identify growth opportunities and make informed strategic decisions.

## 4. Project Objectives

- Measure overall sales performance.
- Identify the highest-performing products.
- Identify the most valuable customers.
- Analyze sales trends over time.
- Compare sales performance across countries.
- Provide business recommendations based on the findings.

## 5. Intended Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest |
|---|---|
| Sales Manager | Improve sales performance |
| Marketing Team | Understand customer behaviour |
| Executive Management | Support strategic decisions |

These are the intended audiences for the analysis, rather than confirmed stakeholders in a client engagement.

## 6. Business Questions

- How much revenue are we generating?
- Which products perform best?
- Which customers are most valuable?
- Which countries contribute most to sales?
- How are sales changing over time?

## 7. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value
- Number of Identifiable Customers
- Revenue by Product
- Quantity Sold by Product
- Revenue by Country
- Monthly Sales
- Top Customers

## 8. Required Data

The project uses the UCI Online Retail transaction dataset.

| Data Element | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Customer IDs | Analyze identifiable customer behaviour |
| Invoice numbers | Analyze orders and identify cancellation invoices |
| Stock codes and product descriptions | Evaluate product performance |
| Quantities and unit prices | Calculate sales value and measure units sold |
| Invoice dates | Analyze sales trends over time |
| Countries | Compare sales performance across countries |
